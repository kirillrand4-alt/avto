Screw compressor

Solid, reliable and efficient, Ceccato screw compressors are a safe investment. They save time, reduce breakdowns, accidents and costs in your production process. Ceccato’s compressors are available with fixed speed, variable speed or permanent magnet motors. We provide direct, gear or belt transmission driven options.

Go to screw compressors

Piston compressors

Piston compressors are ideal for both professionals and hobbyists, who do not require continuous compressed air. Ceccato’s piston compressors are solid, compact and tested to work for many years. Piston compressors are available with wheels or base mounted, with coaxial or belt transmission technology, they can be lubricated or oil-free, diesel or oil engines and come in silent ranges. They come in all shapes and sizes all the way up to 15 bar machines.

Go to piston compressors

Oil-free compressors

Oil-free compressors have been designed for all industrial and professional applications where the quality of the air plays a fundamental role. Ceccato's oil-free compressors are widely used in food and beverage, electronic, chemical and pharmaceutical applications, dentistry, laboratories and medical clinics.

Choosing Ceccato’s oil-free compressors eliminates your oil contamination risk.

Go to oil-free compressors

Boosters

Our bstAIR pressure boosters are ideal for those sectors and processes that require high pressure in compressed air or nitrogen, such as laser cutting, PET blowing or plastic material printing.
